The instrument

Music gives it color.

RG Resonance is a personal reference library for learning how different kinds of music create felt resonance.

Somewhere and Hope To See You Again are sparse, emotionally adhesive reference points. Their official slowed variants create controlled comparisons for pitch, onset spacing, decay, spectral balance, and emotional hang time.

The visual field is generated in the browser from the playing audio. Calibration capture is opt-in through ?calibrate=1; ordinary listening records and uploads nothing.
