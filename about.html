The instrument

Music gives it color.

RG Player is a private personal library whose visual identity comes from the artwork attached to each release.

The player keeps catalog metadata, delivery providers, and rights provenance separate. A library can move from bundled files to external storage without redesigning playback or losing source history.

The visual field is generated in the browser from the playing audio. Calibration capture is opt-in through ?calibrate=1; ordinary listening records and uploads nothing.

The bundled Signal Study artwork and calibration pulse were created for this repository and exist only to verify the player without copying an artist catalog.
